Privacy

How this website handles information

This portfolio does not have an account system or contact form, and it does not use analytics, advertising trackers or cookies. It does not store anything about you in your browser.

External services

The site loads typefaces from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those fonts, Google may receive standard connection information such as your IP address. You can disable external font loading in your browser; the site will use a system font instead.

When you contact Dennis

The email and WhatsApp links open your email app or WhatsApp. If you choose to send a message, the information you include is handled by that service and received by Dennis. Review the relevant service’s privacy information before sending details you consider sensitive. The “copy email” button only copies the address to your clipboard on your own device.
